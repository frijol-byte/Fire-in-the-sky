# 05 — Lore Primer (DRAFT, owner approval needed)

> Everything here is a **proposal** (decision D5). Names are placeholders until the owner
> approves them. A name search (trademarks, other games) happens in the naming task before
> anything goes public. The lore is fully original: nothing from GoF2/Everspace.

## 1. Premise (proposed)

Humanity has spread across a few dozen systems, held together by the **Solar Concord**, a
federation whose navy is stretched thin at the frontier. Commerce is run by the **Meridian
Guild**, and the gaps between them are filled by the **Cinder Clans**, pirate families who
raid convoys and run the fringe.

Beyond the frontier live the **Ithari**, an ancient, crystal-tech species of
merchant-pilgrims who tolerate humans but don't trust them.

For the past few years, frontier colonies have reported the same thing before they stop
answering: **fire in the sky**. Silent auroral sheets of light spread across the stars,
followed by a complete loss of signal. The Concord calls it a solar phenomenon. The Ithari
have an older word for what comes after the fire: **the Hush**.

**You** are a freelance pilot (name TBD, possibly player-chosen) whose ship's black box
recorded a fire-in-the-sky event at a colony that no longer exists. You don't remember most
of that night. Your ship barely made it out. The recording is now the most valuable thing you
own, and the Concord, the Guild, the Clans and the Ithari would all like to have it.

## 2. Title tie-in
"Fire in the Sky" is the in-world name for the phenomenon that comes before the Hush. It
shows up as the slice's ambient mystery (sky flickers in Ilu'Vash, the Silent Wreck) and
becomes the campaign's central thread. (The title is still a working title; the phenomenon
can keep the name even if the game is renamed.)

## 3. Factions

### Solar Concord (human federation & navy)
- Bureaucratic, proud, overstretched. Means well, hides things.
- Gameplay: friendly patrols, military outpost, bounty contracts, a reputation-gated
  military equipment shop (backlog).
- Voice: clipped military radio talk.

### Meridian Guild (human traders)
- Corporate merchant houses. Pragmatic, money first, with a surprisingly strong code of
  contract honor.
- Gameplay: trade hubs, delivery and escort jobs, the best markets.
- Voice: friendly and transactional.

### Cinder Clans (pirates)
- Raider families. Each clan is led by a warlord. The slice's warlord, **"Matriarch" Saska
  Vey**, flies the stolen Concord carrier that becomes the slice boss.
- Gameplay: main enemies, elites with callsigns, bounty chains.
- Voice: swaggering, taunting radio barks.

### Ithari (alien race 1, slice)
- Tall, slow-spoken, crystal-symbiotic beings who "grow" their ships from resonant crystal.
  Merchant-pilgrims who travel between ancient relay sites.
- Neutral. Humans have to earn their trust. They know more about the Hush than they say.
- Gameplay: reputation-gated station, unique goods (Ithari Glass, Spice, Crystal), one
  purchasable ship at Respected, blueprints at Honored.
- Voice: formal, metaphor-heavy, refers to people by their deeds ("the one who spared the
  convoy").

### The Hush (mystery threat)
- **What players know in the slice:** fire-in-the-sky events, silent colonies, the Silent
  Wreck, Ithari rumors.
- **Writer's notes (secret, for consistency, draft):** the Hush is a dormant intelligence
  whose "fire" is a kind of scan. Places that "answer" the scan are silenced. Ithari relay
  sites were built to stay quiet. The player's black box holds a fragment of the scan, which
  is why echo copies appear at the Silent Wreck. *(This is open for the owner to replace.)*

### Backlog
- **Alien race 2:** militaristic and expansionist, pushing into human space because they are
  fleeing the Hush.
- **Minor species (3–4):** traders, scavengers, a symbiotic species living on Ithari
  ships, etc.

## 4. Slice prologue (3 missions, also the tutorial)
1. **"Static"**: you wake up in a damaged ship drifting near Haldane's jumpgate, with
   fragments of the black box replaying. Teaches flight, FA-off drift and boost. A Concord
   patrol escorts you in.
2. **"Debts"**: dock at Haldane Prime. Your ship is impounded until you pay for repairs.
   Teaches docking, the market and a first delivery job.
3. **"Sparks"**: pirates ambush the delivery and are after your recording. Teaches combat,
   targeting and missiles. You survive and are now on the Cinder Clans' radar. The bounty
   chain against the Matriarch opens. The free loop begins.

## 5. Writing & tone guide
- **Space opera tone:** earnest stakes, warm characters, a bit of humor in radio chatter.
  Never grimdark, never parody.
- **Mystery is shown, not explained.** Hint through sky effects, sound, rumors and logs.
  Save the answers for the campaign.
- **Radio barks:** short (≤ 8 words), faction-flavored, never more than one at a time, and
  they also telegraph AI intent.
- **Naming rules:** pronounceable, no real trademarks, nothing from GoF2/Everspace/Elite/Star
  Wars/etc. Human names are a multicultural mix. Ithari names use apostrophes and soft
  consonants (Ilu'Vash, Sae'rin). Pirate callsigns are vivid nicknames ("Ashjaw", "Two-Knives").
- All text goes through localization keys and must stay under the UI length limits.

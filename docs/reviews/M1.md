## Findings

### Fixed in this gate
- **F1. Mouse flight was dead in the flight arena (and the debug flight scene).** Both capture
  the mouse (`Input.MOUSE_MODE_CAPTURED`), and a captured cursor stays at the window centre, so
  `InputRouter`'s virtual stick ("cursor offset from the centre") was always zero. Confirmed
  under Xvfb: the cursor position stayed at the exact centre while 120 px of mouse motion
  arrived. Fix: `InputRouter` adds up mouse motion into the stick, held at the max radius so
  moving back eases off at once (`accumulate_mouse_offset`), uses it whenever the cursor is
  captured (`virtual_stick_offset`), and recentres it when you pick up the gamepad and on every
  respawn (`recenter_mouse`). The HUD draws the deadzone ring and a dot where the stick points.
  Seven new unit tests (`test_input_mouse_capture.gd`); verified live under Xvfb with `xdotool`
  (the ship turns right and up with the mouse).
- **F2. The HUD read the ship's raw physics position while the camera reads the smoothed one.**
  Physics interpolation is on (M1-T5), and architecture §5.1 says anything that follows a ship
  each frame must read `get_global_transform_interpolated()`. The camera did; the HUD's nose ring
  didn't, so on a monitor faster than 60 Hz it would jitter against the view during turns.
  The HUD now uses the interpolated transform.
- **F3. STATUS** showed M1-T7 as "PR open" (merged in #16) and the `playtest-M0` release as
  unpublished (published 2026-10-18).

### Noted, no action needed now
- The flight arena turns the `camera/free_look` setting on for everyone who enters it, and it
  stays on after leaving (only in memory; nothing saves settings yet). Fine while the right
  stick has nothing to aim at; the options menu (M8-T7) owns this setting.
- The pause menu's Quit exits the game rather than returning to the title. It's labelled a
  stand-in until the game-flow work.
- The engine glow is the same blue-white on every ship (a constant in `EngineFx`). Moving it into
  `ShipDef` is now part of M2-T7 (pirates need their own colour). The Drayman borrows the light
  fighter's canopy until M8-T1's "human heavy" canopy.
- `CREDITS.md` is hand-written; the art bible says it's generated from `licenses.csv`. That's
  already part of M8-T6 and harmless while every asset is our own.
- At 7,996 triangles with every tier part, the Drayman sits at the top of the fighter budget
  (3–8 k). It's a 35 m freighter, so that's reasonable, but any new parts need a LOD or a trim.

## Design questions (for you)
1. **Paddle-only actions.** Vertical thrust (M1-T1) and free look (M1-T4) are bound to back
   paddles on the gamepad, which a standard Xbox pad lacks (every other button is taken). Options:
   leave as is (keyboard R/F/Z still work), or give them a "hold a button + stick" combination
   in a later card. I left them as they are; your playtest will tell whether you miss them.

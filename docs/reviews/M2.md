# M2 review gate: Combat core

**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Reviewed:** PRs #20–#26 (M2-T1 … M2-T7), `main` at `4e02530`
**Verdict:** ✅ **Gate clean** after one real fix (F1: the combat arena could not be reached in the
game). M2 is ready for your playtest (`playtest-M2` release).

## Summary for the owner

All seven combat cards are merged and do what their cards asked. I re-ran everything in a fresh
cloud session:

- **Every test passes:** 711 unit tests (707 before this gate, plus 4 new ones), 456 simulation
  tests including the long ones, 27 tool tests, 46 Blender pipeline tests, "boot every scene" for
  15 scenes, data validation, format and lint.
- **CI is green on `main`** for the last merge (#26), both the build and the asset check.
- **The sims test the fighting itself:** shots hit a moving dummy at the predicted time; guns heat
  up, overheat and draw energy; 10,000 shots reuse the pool without leaks; a missile hits a target
  that flies straight and misses one that boost-drifts sideways at the right moment; flares fool
  missiles at their set rate (2,000 seeded rolls); an AI kills a dummy in under a minute in every
  ship; AI vs AI duels end with a winner in under 3 minutes; the AI hits rocks no more than 3 times
  a minute; destroyed ships explode, drop loot and the tractor collects it; dying and respawning
  restarts the wave.
- **Balance:** an "ace" AI flying your Kestrel through the combat test clears wave 1 (13 s,
  untouched) and wave 2 (52 s, hull at 92%), and **dies in wave 3**. A person with aim assist,
  missiles and flares should do better than that stand-in, but this is the main question for your
  playtest: "Is 1v3 tense but winnable?"
- **Nothing generated was hand-edited:** all 20 sounds rebuild byte for byte and CI's asset check
  rebuilds all models. Every sound has its licence row (all synthesised by our own script, CC0).
- **Screenshots:** I rendered the M2 shot scenes and the title screen and looked at them (list
  below).

**One real bug, fixed:** **you could not get to the combat test in the game.** The title screen
only had "Y: fly the flight test arena", and nothing led to the new combat arena. The tests
start that scene directly, so no earlier session noticed. The title screen now offers both:
**A** (left click) = fight pirates in the combat test, **Y** (G) = the flight test arena. I
checked it end to end: from the title screen, pressing A loads the combat arena, and the first
wave arrives on its own.

**What you do next:** merge this PR, publish the `playtest-M2` release from GitHub's Releases page
(owner quickstart §5), press **A** on the title screen and play with a gamepad for ~25 minutes.
The checklist is at the bottom of `docs/tasks/M2-combat-core.md`, and the controls are in the
release notes. Two things to know before you play:
- To compare the **laser and the autocannon** you need a keyboard: **F3** switches you to the
  Bulwark (autocannon + rockets), **F2** back to the Kestrel (laser + homing missiles).
- If a wave is too hard, **F7** (keyboard) opens a wave menu to pick, restart or skip waves.

## Findings

### Fixed in this gate
- **F1. The combat test arena could not be reached in a build.** `Game` only knew the flight test
  scene and the title screen only answered `interact` (Y). The M2 playtest checklist plays the
  combat test with a gamepad, so the playtest was impossible. Fix: `Game.go_to_combat_test()`;
  the title screen sends **A** (`fire_primary`, left click on a keyboard) to the combat test and
  keeps **Y** for the flight arena, and shows both hints. The pure rule `Boot.destination_for(event)`
  has four new unit tests. The boot screen now reads `_input`, because its full-screen root
  `Control` swallows mouse clicks before `_unhandled_input`. Checked end to end in a headless run
  (title → A → combat arena → wave 1 with one pirate).
- **F2. STATUS** listed M2-T5…T7 and M1-F1 as "PR open" (all merged), the "Now" section was still
  about M1, and the M2-T1…T7 notes had been added *below* the "Release notes" heading. That last one
  would have pasted all of them into the `playtest-M1` release notes if it were rebuilt. Moved them back
  under "Notes for the next session", and added the `playtest-M2` release notes (controls included).

### Noted, no action needed now
- **Ship switching and the wave menu are keyboard only** (F2–F4, F5–F7). Fine for a test arena;
  ship choice becomes the hangar in M4/M5.
- **Y is both "target nearest" and "interact"** (interact does nothing in space until docking in
  M4). M4 must split them or make interact context-sensitive.
- **"Look at target" (B key) is on back paddle 4 on the gamepad**, like free look. A standard Xbox
  pad can't use it (same open question as the M1 gate's paddle actions).
- **The AI only sees rocks near you:** rock colliders exist only around the player's ship, so a
  pirate far away cannot hit a rock (and does not need to dodge one). Harmless now; written into
  the M3-T1 card.
- **`ai_pilot.gd` is large (~590 lines).** M3-T1's card now asks for new behaviors in their own
  scripts.
- **A few AI tuning constants are in code** (`AIPilot`: sight cone, rocket cone, energy thresholds).
  They aren't content, but if M3 wants them per profile they move to `AIProfileDef`.
- **The encounter director lives in `combat/`**; architecture §2 puts it under `ai/`. M3-T6 moves
  it when it grows the director.
- **The F5 "pirate" in the flight arena is a Kestrel** (same model and name as your ship), so its kill banner and
  the death screen read "Kestrel". The combat test uses the real Cinder Clans ships; the flight arena's F5 helper
  can switch to `pirate_fighter` whenever a session touches it.
- **The flight arena's help text is tall** and its first lines sit close to the readout; the combat test's is fine.
  The help text belongs to the test arenas, not the game's HUD.
- **Wave progress, wallet and kills are not saved** (no save system until later); the wallet keeps
  growing across deaths until insurance arrives in M4.

## Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1v3 is too hard for a person (the AI stand-in loses wave 3) | Medium | Medium | Playtest decides; soften the wave data, not the code |
| Combat feel (hit sounds, explosion size, shake) disappoints on a real GPU and speakers | Medium | Medium | All screenshots are software-rendered and all sounds are synthesised placeholders; the art/audio pass is M8, but quick numbers live in `feedback_default.tres`, `destruction_default.tres` and the weapon defs |
| Aim assist strength wrong | Medium | Low | One number per difficulty (`data/difficulty/*.tres`) plus the targeting def |
| Many ships + shots in one fight cost frame rate | Low–medium | Medium | Fixed pools; the ≤ 14 ships budget is far away (combat test peaks at 4 ships); perf smoke test in M9 |
| Packed arrays in defs come out empty in exported builds (happened twice in M2) | Medium | High | Caught by CI's export check; written into the M3 cards and STATUS: use typed `Array[...]` |
